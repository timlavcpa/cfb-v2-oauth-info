Privacy Policy

CFB V2 Dashboard

Purpose

CFB V2 Dashboard is a personal application used to generate college football analysis reports and store spreadsheet files in the application owner's Google Drive.

Information Accessed

With the owner's authorization, the application accesses Google Drive to upload and manage its generated reports.

How Information Is Used

Google Drive access is used solely to store and manage the application's spreadsheet reports. Google account information is not sold or used for advertising.

Data Sharing

The application does not sell or share Google user data with third parties for advertising or marketing.

Data Security

Google authorization credentials are intended to be stored securely and are not published on this website.

Revoking Access

The owner can revoke the application's Google account access through Google Account security settings.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the application owner through the project's GitHub repository.

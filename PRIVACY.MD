# Privacy Policy — CFB V2 Dashboard

**Effective Date:** October 28, 2026

## 1. Overview

CFB V2 Dashboard is a personal-use application designed to automate the transfer of college football analytics reports from GitHub Actions to an authorized Google Drive account.

The application is not offered as a commercial service.

## 2. Information Accessed

With the Google account owner's authorization, the application may access Google Drive information necessary to locate the designated report folder and upload or manage generated Excel reports.

The application does not require access to Gmail, contacts, calendars, or unrelated Google services.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Google Drive access is used exclusively to:

- Identify the authorized destination folder.
- Upload generated CFB V2 Excel workbooks.
- Update or organize reports within that folder as necessary.

Google user information is not used for advertising, profiling, or marketing.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

OAuth credentials and authorization tokens are intended to be stored securely using GitHub Actions encrypted secrets.

Credentials will not be published in the application's public repository.

Access is limited to the permissions authorized by the Google account owner.

## 5. Information Sharing

The application does not sell, rent, or share Google user data with advertisers or data brokers.

Google Drive information is processed only as necessary to perform the authorized synchronization.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Generated reports remain in the authorized Google Drive account until the account owner deletes them.

The account owner may revoke the application's Google account access at any time through Google Account security settings.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data-handling practices change.

## 8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner using the support contact provided in the Google OAuth application configuration.
